# Synthetic speech fixtures

Russian and Ukrainian PCM16 mono at 16 kHz, generated locally for voice QA. No human recording, credentials or customer data. The acceptance test mixes deterministic pink noise at SNR 20/15/10/5 dB and checks completion, not Soniox transcription accuracy. Two voices and phrases do not constitute full device/language acceptance.

Short-command fixtures: ru «Да» (Milena), uk «Ні» (Lesya), generated by macOS speech synthesis. Short noisy cases continue the fixture background for two additional seconds; this does not add clean silence.
